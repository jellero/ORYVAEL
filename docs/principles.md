# Engineering Principles

1. **Intelligence is not authority.** Model capability never implies privilege.
2. **Deny by default.** Privileged actions need explicit authorization.
3. **Constitutional controls are outside AI authority.**
4. **No self-approval.** Producer cannot be sole verifier/releaser.
5. **Evidence before deployment.**
6. **Fail closed.** Mandatory protection never silently degrades.
7. **Deterministic policy.** LLMs may propose rules, never interpret final authorization.
8. **Minimal trusted computing base.**
9. **Observe independently.** A component cannot be sole judge of its own health.
10. **Reversible state.** Updates preserve rollback unless explicit high-risk approval says otherwise.
11. **Complexity has a budget.**
12. **Provenance is part of the artifact.**
13. **System AI and app AI follow the same security principles.**
14. **Privacy boundaries precede model calls.**
15. **Humans retain an independent recovery path.**
