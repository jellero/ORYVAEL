# Human Control Model

Human control does not mean manually reviewing every AI-generated line. Human attention is focused on authority and risk boundaries.

Humans define:
- system intent;
- constitutional constraints;
- change classes;
- root trust;
- privacy;
- architecture;
- maximum autonomous rollout.

Automation produces implementation, repetitive review, tests, evidence, monitoring and diagnosis.

## Governance console

Should surface:
- pending C3/C4 changes;
- capability expansions;
- dependency expansion;
- mandatory verification failures;
- rollout state;
- incidents;
- root-key operations.

## Human emergency controls

Must support:
- disable principal;
- revoke capability;
- freeze releases;
- force rollback;
- enter recovery;
- rotate keys.

These controls must not depend solely on the AI orchestration service.

## Approval quality

A privilege approval shows the material delta: old/new capabilities, affected principals/data, duration, scope and verifier results. Generic consent without context is insufficient.
