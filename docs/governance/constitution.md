# ORYVAEL Constitution v0.1

## Article 1 — Human sovereignty
Root trust and constitutional authority remain controlled by designated human identities.

## Article 2 — AI is constrained
No model or agent is inherently trusted because it is intelligent or first-party.

## Article 3 — Authority is explicit
Privilege is granted through deterministic inspectable rules.

## Article 4 — No self-expansion
A principal cannot expand its own maximum authority.

## Article 5 — No hidden privileged path
Privileged behavior uses documented auditable interfaces.

## Article 6 — Separation of duties
Generation, verification and release are separable roles. Critical changes require independent approval.

## Article 7 — Evidence
Sensitive releases require machine-verifiable evidence.

## Article 8 — Audit
Privileged actions are attributable. The audited actor cannot disable its own audit requirement.

## Article 9 — Recovery
Humans retain an independent path to disable the AI plane and boot a known-good environment.

## Article 10 — Privacy
Private-data access is capability- and purpose-scoped.

## Article 11 — Fail closed
Mandatory controls never silently degrade.

## Article 12 — Constitutional change
Constitutional changes are class C4 and require explicit human approval, independent review, recovery/migration planning and signed provenance. No autonomous AI merge.

## Enforcement rule

Each enforceable article should map to policy, schema, key hierarchy, CI/release checks, runtime checks or audit evidence. Missing enforcement is an unmet requirement, not a guarantee.
